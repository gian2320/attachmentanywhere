CARD SPECIFICATIONS NTAG213 / NTAG215

NFC Executive Cards

Technical specifications and build details for Attachment Anywhere contactless business cards.

Chip & Memory
NXP NTAG213 (144 bytes) or NTAG215 (504 bytes). Pre-programmed or writeable with any standard NFC app.
Device Compatibility
Native tap on iPhone (XS & newer) and all NFC Android phones. No apps or camera scanning required.
Card Build & Size
Standard credit card dimensions (85.6 × 54 mm, 0.84 mm thick). Matte black PVC finish with waterproof seal.
Durability & Life
Waterproof (IP68), scratch-resistant, 100,000 rewrites, and 10-year EEPROM data retention.
